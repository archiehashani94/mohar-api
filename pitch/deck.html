Insurers pay. Diagnoses stay sealed.

Health-insurance claims proven with zero knowledge, settled by a smart contract in seconds. The insurer never sees the diagnosis, and the payment never reveals which hospital was paid.

Hackasol 5.0 · Track 4 Web3 · W3-01Noir · UltraHonk · Solidity · IPFS · FHIR · Zcash
The problem

To get a claim paid, you hand a stranger your medical history.

Privacy

Diagnoses, prescriptions, psychiatric and reproductive care pile up in insurer databases the patient can't see or control.

Hours

IRDAI's 2024 master circular gives insurers 3 hours to clear a cashless discharge. Patients still wait at the billing desk while someone reads the file.

Fraud

Unlicensed clinics, phantom patients, duplicate bills and claims split to slip under limits, checked by hand.

The problem statement: a hospital must prove a claim is valid, a contract must settle it, and the insurer must never see the medical details.

How it works

One claim, Patient → Hospital → Insurer, in under a minute.

1

Hospital seals the encounter

FHIR R4 record AES-encrypted, pinned to IPFS. Consent request sealed to the patient's key.

2

Patient's phone checks, then signs

Re-derives payout, claim ID and digest from its own policy copy, then signs the exact terms.

3

Proof generated in the hospital's browser

Noir circuit, UltraHonk via bb.js. Seven statements, 45.6k gates. Nothing medical uploaded.

4

Relayer submits, contract verifies

On-chain verifier, nullifier, policy usage, date window, pool balance.

5

Ghost Pay

bINR to keccak(secret, nullifier), a one-time address only the hospital can link. Or ZEC via the shielded pool.

6

Insurer sees an amount + six ✓

No human approval. No medical file. Settled before discharge.
